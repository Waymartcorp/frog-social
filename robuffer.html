This is the facility-standard remineralization / conductivity buffer for RO water. Use this card during troubleshooting and when onboarding new staff.

Purpose

  • Stabilize water conductivity and reduce stress
  • Standardize “what water means” across rooms, racks, and institutions
  • Make troubleshooting measurable (conductivity targets + repeatable mixing)

Mixing steps

  1. Fill container to ~80–90% final volume with RO water.
  2. Add salts, mix until fully dissolved.
  3. Top up to final volume.
  4. Measure conductivity with a calibrated meter.
  5. Log: date/time, batch, target vs measured conductivity, who mixed it.

Common mistakes

  • Uncalibrated meters (false “mystery problems”).
  • Chasing pH instead of fixing conductivity / buffering consistency.
  • Changing multiple variables at once during troubleshooting.
  • Lot-to-lot salt differences without logging.

When ROBUFFER is suspect

  • Sudden behavior changes across many tanks/racks
  • Feeding suppression + lethargy after water change
  • Chronic low-level stress signs with no visible lesions
