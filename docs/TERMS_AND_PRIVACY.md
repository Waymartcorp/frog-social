# Frog Social — Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

## 1. Content Ownership and Use Protection

### 1.1 User-Generated Content Rights

All content posted on Frog Social remains the intellectual property of the original poster.

By posting on Frog Social, users grant Frog Social a limited license to:
- display the content within the platform
- use the content for internal case formation, emerging thread detection, and AI-guided recommendations within the platform
- store the content as part of Case History and platform memory

Users do **not** grant permission for:
- reposting outside Frog Social
- redistribution to third parties
- public attribution outside the platform
- use in external publications without explicit written permission from the poster
- scraping, mirroring, or bulk export of user-generated content

### 1.2 No External Redistribution Without Permission

User content may **not** be reposted, redistributed, quoted, or publicly attributed outside Frog Social without explicit permission from the original poster.

This applies to:
- social posts
- case descriptions
- observations
- images and videos
- colony management records
- any identifiable contribution

Violation of this rule may result in account suspension or removal.

### 1.3 Platform Use of Content

Frog Social may use aggregated, anonymized patterns from user content to:
- improve AI recommendations
- shape husbandry guidance
- identify recurring community-wide patterns

This use is always:
- anonymized (no user attribution)
- internal to the platform
- non-commercial unless explicitly disclosed

---

## 2. Login-Gating and Access Control

### 2.1 Platform Access

Frog Social is a login-gated platform.

- All posting, case creation, and colony management requires authentication.
- Browsing public case summaries may be allowed without login (future decision), but contributing content always requires an account.
- No content is indexed by public search engines unless explicitly marked for public visibility.

### 2.2 Account Creation

Accounts may be created with:
- email (primary)
- institutional affiliation (optional)
- display name or coded identity (user's choice)

### 2.3 Data Minimization

Frog Social collects only what is needed:
- authentication credentials
- display identity (name or pseudonym)
- optional institutional affiliation
- content the user chooses to post

Frog Social does **not**:
- sell user data
- share identifiable data with third parties
- require real names (see Anonymity section below)

---

## 3. Anonymity and Privacy Controls

### 3.1 Anonymous Posting

Users may post to the Social feed anonymously.

Anonymous posts:
- display as "Anonymous" or a system-generated identifier
- are still stored with the user's account internally (for moderation)
- cannot be attributed publicly to the poster
- still contribute to emerging threads and case formation

### 3.2 Anonymous Case Posting

Users may submit cases via Describe a Problem anonymously.

Anonymous cases:
- omit the poster's display name from all public views
- still link to the user's account internally
- are fully functional in Case History (search, recall, resolution)
- can be de-anonymized only by the original poster

### 3.3 Colony Registration Privacy

Colony and system registrations default to **private**.

- Only the logged-in user (or authorized group members) can see their colony records.
- Colony identifiers may use coded/pseudonymous names.
- No colony data is shared with other users unless the owner explicitly grants access.
- Institutional affiliation is optional and never displayed without permission.

### 3.4 Coded Identity

Users may register with a coded identity rather than a real name.

- The coded identity is the display name visible to other users.
- Real identity is known only to the user and (if required) platform administrators for moderation.
- Users may change their display identity at any time.

### 3.5 Moderation Access

Platform moderators/stewards may access poster identity only:
- to enforce terms of service
- to respond to abuse reports
- to verify accounts when required by institutional policy

Moderation access is logged and auditable.

---

## 4. Data Retention

- Social posts persist per platform retention rules.
- Cases persist permanently in Case History.
- Colony management records persist as long as the account is active.
- Users may request deletion of their content (subject to reasonable processing time).
- Deletion removes public visibility; anonymized pattern contributions may persist in aggregate form.

---

## 5. Non-Negotiables

1. No external redistribution without poster permission.
2. Login required for all contributions.
3. Anonymous posting and case submission are supported.
4. Colony records are private by default.
5. No real-name requirement.
6. No sale or sharing of identifiable user data.
7. Platform is privacy-conscious by design.
